Help & Support

Where is my order?

Open the Track tab and enter your order ID. The seller updates the status as your order is packed and shipped.

How do I cancel an order?

You can cancel before the seller dispatches it. Raise a ticket below with your order ID.

My plant arrived damaged

Raise a Refund ticket with your order ID and photos within the return window. We will review it with the seller.

When will I get my refund?

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Cash on Delivery refunds need your bank or UPI details.

3. Orders and pricing

All prices are in INR and include applicable taxes unless stated. An order is confirmed when the seller accepts it. Sellers may cancel for stock or delivery issues, with a full refund of any prepaid amount.

4. Payments

We support UPI, cards and Cash on Delivery. Online payments are confirmed only after verification by our payment provider.

5. Delivery

Each seller handles delivery independently. Estimated timelines are shown by the seller. Live plants may vary slightly in size and appearance.

Orders can be cancelled before dispatch. Damaged, wrong or dead-on-arrival items can be reported within the window in the Return & Replacement Policy, with photo proof.
